Data Privacy Policy (RA 10173)

Palista Muna Data Privacy Commitment

In accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) of the Philippines, Palista Muna strictly commits to protecting the personal information of micro-merchants and their customers.


1. Data Collected:

Customer name, mobile number (for merchant-triggered SMS notices), loan amount, items, due date, and credit limits.


2. Security & Storage:

• Custom Store ID Partition: Data is isolated per merchant via their 6-Digit Store ID and 4-Digit PIN.
• No Data Selling: Information is never sold, harvested, or transmitted to third-party ad networks.
• Native SMS Routing: SMS notices open directly on your mobile carrier's native messenger.
